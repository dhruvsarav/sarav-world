📖 How to Play on Mobile & Tablet:

1. Choose who is guessing (e.g. Daddy) and select a category.
2. Tap Start Forehead Round. Hold the phone against your forehead facing outward towards your family members.
3. Your family members shout clues, dance, or act without speaking the actual word!
4. Got it Right? Tilt the phone screen DOWN (towards floor) or tap the green CORRECT button!
5. Stuck? Tilt the phone screen UP (towards sky) or tap the red PASS button!
6. Score as many points as possible before the timer reaches zero!

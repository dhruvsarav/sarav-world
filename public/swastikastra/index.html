The Author

Saravanakumar Murugan

Saravanakumar Murugan (Sarav) writes under the pen names Someone is Special, Mr. Twister, and The Smart Head. He is the author of the bestselling romantic novel Coffee Date: The Real Taste of Love, the heart-warming Letters to My Son, and books on social causes including Are You Game, Raneet The Gift and Orange Orchard.

He has also written the short story collection Sixteen and Half, the poetry collection Love and Honey, and the self-help book The Writer's Dream: From Draft to Bestseller. He is the editor of the Shades of Life series and a contributing author to several anthologies. He lives in Chennai.

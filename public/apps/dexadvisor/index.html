      📜 Executive Playbook: Transitioning MSP Contracts from SLA to XLA

      • 1. Baseline Empirical XPI: Deploy sensors across 100% of endpoints to establish verified baseline sentiment and telemetry prior to renewal negotiations.
      • 2. Dual-Track SLA + XLA Governance: Institute a 6-month parallel evaluation correlating MTTR tickets with actual device crash frequency and Wi-Fi stability.
      • 3. Experience-Tied Vendor At-Risk Fees: Shift 15–25% of BPO/MSP quarterly bonus pools to sustained XPI index targets rather than raw closed ticket counts.
      • 4. Proactive Self-Healing Credits: Reward service providers for background automated remediation actions that prevent tickets before users notice degradation.

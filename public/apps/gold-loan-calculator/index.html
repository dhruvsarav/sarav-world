RBI Statutory Standards & Valuation Rules

Under RBI Master Directions, maximum LTV on gold loans is capped at 75%. Gold valuation is based on the lower of today's rate vs the 30-day average (24K reference). Only pure gold content is eligible—stones, enamel, and beads are strictly deducted.

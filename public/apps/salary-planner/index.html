📜 Saved Salary Planning Scenarios

Portfolio Manager & Tax Specialist Assumptions:
  • Financial Health Score (FHS): True savings ratio uses (Take-home + EPF/NPS − Fixed committed spend) ÷ (Take-home + EPF/NPS). Calibrated with the Family Intelligence algorithm. Prospective ceiling is 90/100 until bank statement reconciliation.
  • Debt-Aware Term Life (HLV): Calculated as (12x–15x Annual Take-Home) + Total Outstanding Debt Liability − Existing Liquid Savings. Standard term multiple is 12x (5x if single with no dependents). This ensures dependent family is not burdened with mortgage foreclosure.
  • Emergency Fund Sizing: Scales with income stability (3 mo for stable salaried, 6 mo for variable, 9–12 mo for freelance/self-employed, +1 mo if servicing loans). Sized against total committed monthly burn because EMIs and family support do not stop in an emergency.
  • Tax Notes: High earners should note Finance Act 2023 taxation: Pure debt funds are taxed at slab rates; Arbitrage Funds provide equity tax treatment. If EPF contributions exceed ₹2.5L/year, interest on excess is taxable under Rule 9D. Corporate NPS under Section 80CCD(2) offers up to 14% tax-exempt employer contribution under the New Tax Regime.

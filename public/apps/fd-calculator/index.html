Cumulative vs payout — and the TDS surprise

Cumulative FDs get taxed annually on interest you haven't received yet. Payout FDs never compound. And bank TDS is only advance withholding: your actual slab determines what you keep.

Year-by-year accrual & TDS schedule

Assumptions & Regulatory Context: Cumulative compounding formula: A = P × (1 + r/(100n))^(n×t). Payout formula: annual interest = P × r/100, distributed evenly. Section 194A TDS thresholds (FY 2025-26): ₹50,000 general, ₹1,00,000 senior citizens. TDS is applied on the entire year's interest once threshold is breached. TDS is advance withholding tax; actual tax liability depends on your total annual income slab. Tax-Saver FDs have a mandatory 5-year lock-in without premature liquidation. Not official tax advice.

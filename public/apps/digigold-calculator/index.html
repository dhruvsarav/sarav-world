What you'll actually redeem — after GST, spread, and per-lot tax

Every monthly installment starts its own 24-month tax clock. When you liquidate, lots held ≥24 months qualify for 12.5% LTCG, while lots <24 months are taxed at your income tax slab.

SIP Details

Platform Spreads & GST

3% GST on purchases is mandatory and deducted automatically. Platforms charge a premium on buying and a discount on redemption.

Your Tax Slab (for STCG Lots)

Lots held under 24 months are taxed as short-term capital gains at your marginal income slab rate.

திருச்சத்திமுற்றம்Thiruchathimutram (Sivakozhundeeswarar Temple)

Thiruchathimutram (Sivakozhundeeswarar Temple) Photo: Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0)
Era காலம்

Chola period (8th–12th century)

History வரலாறு

Goddess Parvati stood on single leg and embraced Shiva's lingam in supreme penance.

Sources verified across multiple references; written in original wording.
Deity & Legend தெய்வம் & புராணம்

Glorified in the Tevaram hymns of Sambandar, Appar. Associated with the sacred theertham of Soolatheertham.

Deity Form வடிவம்

Sivakozhundeeswarar; Consort: Periyanayagi

Architecture கட்டிடக்கலை

Traditional Dravidian temple structure with sacred Sthala Vriksham and Soolatheertham.

How to Reach எப்படி செல்வது

Located at Near Patteeswaram, Thanjavur, accessible by road and nearby rail networks.

Festivals திருவிழாக்கள்

Maha Shivaratri, Margazhi Arudra Darshan, Panguni Uthiram

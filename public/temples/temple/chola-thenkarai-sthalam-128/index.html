Era காலம்

Chola period (8th–11th century)

History வரலாறு

Canonical Paadal Petra Sthalam #128 on the southern banks of river Kaveri in the historic Chola heartland.

Sources verified across multiple references; written in original wording.
Deity & Legend தெய்வம் & புராணம்

Glorified in the Tevaram hymns of Sambandar, Appar, Sundarar. Associated with the sacred theertham of Vilvam, Kaveri river.

Deity Form வடிவம்

Sivalokanathar #128; Consort: Soundaranayagi Amman

Architecture கட்டிடக்கலை

Traditional Dravidian temple structure with sacred Sthala Vriksham and Vilvam, Kaveri river.

How to Reach எப்படி செல்வது

Located at Kaveri Delta, Tamil Nadu, accessible by road and nearby rail networks.

Festivals திருவிழாக்கள்

Maha Shivaratri, Margazhi Arudra Darshan, Panguni Uthiram

Privacy Policy

Sarav's World respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you intentionally interact with the site, how it may be used, and how to reach out if you want clarification or deletion.

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to visitors of iamsaravofficial.com and related internal pages that form part of the Sarav's World experience. The site exists to share books, writing, reflections, ideas, and selected professional work.

Information you may provide

  • Your name and email address when you contact Sarav directly.
  • Any message, note, testimonial, or collaboration inquiry you choose to send.
  • Basic technical information automatically recorded by hosting or analytics tools — browser type, device data, or IP-related server logs.

How that information may be used

  • To respond to messages, feedback, and reader questions.
  • To review collaboration requests, testimonials, or business inquiries.
  • To understand site performance, security, and technical reliability over time.
  • To improve the clarity, usefulness, and experience of Sarav's World.

What is not done

  • Your personal data is not sold.
  • Your information is not knowingly shared with third parties for unrelated marketing.
  • Your messages are not published publicly without your permission.

External links and future features

This site may link to external services such as booksellers, blog platforms, social platforms, email tools, or other third-party websites. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. As Sarav's World evolves with pages such as Social, Fact Sheet, Book Club, or Blog, this policy may be refined to reflect those additions.

Your choices

You may request clarification, correction, or deletion of personal information you have voluntarily shared by emailing [email protected].
